Package pledgeunveil - pledge(2) and unveil(2) on OpenBSD, no-ops elsewhere

FUNCTIONS

func MultiUnveil(toUnveil [][2]string) error
    MultiUnveil wraps Unveil to unveil the {path, permissions} pairs in
    toUnveil. Paths may be the empty string; if so the path and corresponding
    perms are silently skipped. UnveilBlock is called if all of the calls to
    Unveil succeed.

func MustPledge(promises string)
    MustPledge Calls Pledge(promises, ""). It panics on error.
